# Ethics & Legal Compliance

NarcoX is built with a strict adherence to civil liberties and data protection laws.

## 1. Lawful Use Only
This tool is strictly designed for authorized law enforcement agencies. It is not for public vigilantism.

## 2. Scope & Privacy (No E2E Decryption)
**End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE) chats and private WhatsApp messages are strictly out of scope.** The system only ingests data from public groups, channels, or accounts where users have no reasonable expectation of privacy, or through lawful interception where authorized.

## 3. Data Minimization & DPDP Act
In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and IT Act, we only store data directly relevant to a flagged offense. 

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop
AI is used for triage, not enforcement. A human investigator MUST verify the evidence package before any action or warrant is requested.

## 5. Bias and False Positives
Our lexicon is continuously audited to prevent false positives (e.g., medical discussions of pharmaceuticals).
