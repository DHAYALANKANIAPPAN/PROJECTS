Privacy Policy - NarcoX

NarcoX is a hackathon prototype built for law enforcement intelligence purposes (Techraga 26).

NarcoX collects and processes messages, comments, and associated metadata (usernames, timestamps, and message content) from connected Telegram and Instagram accounts solely for the purpose of detecting narcotics-related activity, as part of an academic hackathon demonstration.

Data collected is used only for research and demonstration purposes and is not shared with third parties. Data may be deleted upon request by contacting the development team.

This tool does not access private end-to-end encrypted communications and operates only on accounts and content explicitly authorized for testing.
