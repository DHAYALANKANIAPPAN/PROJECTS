How this works: real road geometry from an OSRM-compatible routing service, sun azimuth/elevation from a standard low-precision solar position formula, sampled along every road segment.
Assumptions: departure time is read in your device's current timezone, not the origin's local time. Timing across the route assumes a roughly even pace (no live traffic). Bus/train use road heading as a proxy for the physical route. This is a planning aid, not a guarantee — clouds, terrain, and buildings all block the sun too.
